Old Faithful data. Probability and statistics

On 26 September 2026 Probability and Statistics received twenty-nine drawn figures, one for most sections and subsections, and its seven earlier Matplotlib figures were redrawn in the same style under their original file names (marked Redrawn). Each figure is drawn from the stated model with D3 and typeset with KaTeX, then rendered to PNG at twice its CSS size by headless Chromium; the source of each is Sources/Figure code/js/src/<name>.js (see Reproducing the figures). Random draws use the seeded linear congruential generator in js/lib/prob.js, with Box–Muller Normal draws, so each figure is reproducible exactly. Their individual draws therefore differ from those of the NumPy examples in the chapter; the prediction-interval figure is the exception, and uses the data of its NumPy example. Simulation illustrations do not replace the assumptions or coverage guarantees stated in the notes.
