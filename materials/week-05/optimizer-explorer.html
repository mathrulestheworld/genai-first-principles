Optimizer Explorer

Pick a two-parameter loss and a few optimizers, then move the sliders: each optimizer’s path and loss curve are recomputed as you drag.

The idea and the interface follow Gabriel Goh, “Why Momentum Really Works”, Distill (2017); its contour, path and slider designs are adapted here under CC BY 4.0.
